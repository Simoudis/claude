## How this file overrides the pack

The pack is built for growth accounts: hook formulas, keyword CTAs, follow
bait. This account is not that. Where the pack and this file disagree, this
file wins.

- **Line one is an observation with stakes,** not a hook formula. Begin from a
  recognizable condition, then reveal the mechanism beneath it. `/ig-reel`
  and `/ig-carousel` may use a formula's structure only if the result reads
  as an observation; never "stop scrolling", "nobody tells you", "here's why".
- **No keyword CTA. No "Comment X".** At most one quiet ask: a save, or a
  question that helps the reader notice something. Often none. When
  `caption.py` warns about a missing ask, that is intended; say so in the
  receipt.
- **No motivational endings, no engagement bait, no performed vulnerability.**
- **Idea before audience.** Decide whether the thought is true and
  structurally sound before shaping it for the feed. Audience sharpens the
  form; it never sets the premise.
- **Not every observation becomes a post.** A skill may recommend not posting.

## My positions

From *Moving. Still.* Things some of my audience does not yet believe:

1. Momentum is not direction. Motion can start making decisions that should
   belong to judgment.
2. The goal is not certainty. It is bearings.
3. Timing comes before strategy. Urgency must not acquire authority.
4. Growth amplifies what already exists, so integrity comes before growth.
5. Self-authored direction does not require narration or an audience.

Never offer a shortcut to certainty, a manufactured arrival, or speed,
visibility and scale as the measure of success. Those contradict the book.
